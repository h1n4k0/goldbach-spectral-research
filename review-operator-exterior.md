# Independent checks of the two actual exterior-operator reductions

These are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review. Both notes retain the true Hardy coordinate realization and use local GRH(N), not global auxiliary Dirichlet GRH.

1. **Microscopic input, all exterior fine modes.** [Proof](r20-operator.md), SHA-256 `2702231741bc387daa5e382cdef95c576a962a6ad6bb45fe8b3d742ad4361089`. Subtracting the cumulative at the actual left endpoint of every physical cell removes precisely the $r_N\mathbf1_{I_j}$ coarse direction. Each low integer has $O(D)$ partners in that high cell, and both signed Beta line families give the claimed increment bound without summing unweighted cell norms. The low edge integral $\int_0^{U/N}w_N^{-1}\,dx$ is $O(U/N)$ even near zero; the high integral weighted by $y^{-2}$ extends to infinity. On unit cells and $U\le Y=N^\gamma$ this error is $O((\log N)/\sqrt N)=o(\delta_N)$. The exact diagonal and the signed Beta model are smaller on the entire micro–high crossing, including coarse directions. The one-sided strip shift error is $O_\kappa((\log N)^2(NY)^{-\kappa})$, with $1/[2(1+\gamma)]<\kappa<1/2$, hence $o(\delta_N)$. Uniformity uses the fixed-distance GRH derivative bound and the removable imprimitive Euler-factor log derivatives of size $O_\kappa(\sum_{p\mid N}\log p)$. Undoing log measure gives $y r_N(y)$; odd character averages produce the stated conjugated cell coefficients and matrix $G=C C^*$ with overall factor $-2\pi/N$. The index set is finite, but its entries sum over infinitely many cells. No claim of a computed matrix or norm cancellation is made.
2. **Exact weighted bridge and near-cell trace bound.** [Proof](r21-operator.md), SHA-256 `364c7e5cbfaf72f8714c4ccd708ed9054c76d36f18cd50059e841343681c2cdf`. The sampled columns use the cumulative at the actual cell endpoint with the same two residue signs and physical normalization. Their approximation of the true crossing is in Hilbert–Schmidt norm. Frozen cumulative tails vanish at each fixed $N$ by $y^{-\alpha}\log^2(2Ny)$ after the weight is integrated, so the centered bridge covariance is a trace-norm limit of simultaneous square cutoffs. Its coefficients are exactly $W_{\max(k,\ell)}-W_k W_\ell/W_{-1}$. The uniform Mangoldt-square progression bound uses Brun–Titchmarsh for primes and the root count in unit groups of prime powers for fixed proper-power exponents, with the longer exponents bounded absolutely. Weighted dyadic summation yields $\sum_k W_k\|b_k\|^2\ll_\gamma \mathfrak S(N)(U/N)(\log N)^2$. Summing absolute rank-one norms on the near band is then legitimate. The norm comparison takes square roots before adding the actual crossing approximation, so it does not multiply a small linear perturbation by an uncontrolled large norm. R-scale deletion requires $U(D+1)=o(\log N)$; no such claim at polynomial $U$. The separated signed covariance and removed single coarse direction remain unestimated.

Neither note controls the full exterior, the full true operator, or Hypotheses S and R.
