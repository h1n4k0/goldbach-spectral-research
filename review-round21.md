# Independent checks of both mixed-sector ends and their joint consequence

These are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review. The three files are independent of any numerical or proxy spectrum.

1. **Lower $e$ endpoint on the larger prefix.** [Proof](r21-source.md), SHA-256 `21a638f5e2a5e1a913f65d5669f676aa87d4a195442a3d4aca2916fc9d8ddaec`. The reciprocal weighted character input follows from the global-GRH subpower bound with uniform conductor, plus Mellin inversion of an integer-exact smoothed indicator. Its transform has $L^1$ loss $O(N^\epsilon\sqrt{1+|\lambda|/C})$ by Plancherel on the stationary range and integration by parts off it. The Möbius-square error majorant has a real principal pole: its Ramanujan coefficient is retained and is $O_\epsilon(N^\epsilon(a,\nu h)/a)$, since the local factor is coprime to $a$. The $a$-dyad sum then costs only a divisor function. CRT, all unit divisors $r\mid N$, reciprocal phase with its hard endpoint, and the exact one-interval product condition are preserved. The raw bound peaks at exponent $551/600$, giving rectangle exponent $-1/150$ and Hilbert–Schmidt exponent $-1/300$ at $b=N^{3/40}$. There is no claim for $e>N^{3/5}$ from these chosen parameters.
2. **Very long $e$ endpoint.** [Proof](r21-arithmetic-high-e.md), SHA-256 `85cf42fa0ba156594025eee68aeeab450eaeaa6590fe0873400814c1ba30332c`. Global GRH supplies uniform reciprocal bounds including principal and imprimitive Euler factors. Perron at a half-integer and character averaging give twisted Mertens on a reduced class; nonreduced classes are reduced by factoring out the fixed gcd and excluding its prime factors. With $d$ and the rough-side cofactor $\ell$ fixed, the exact Boolean mask and CRT give either an empty set or one residue class for $e$, cut by a single hard interval. Abel with the original log marker and summation over $d\ll D(\log N)^2$ and $\ell\le Z/E$ give raw $O_\epsilon(DZE^{-1/2}N^\epsilon)$ per line. The square-root Hardy transfer gives $b^{7/6}N^{1/6+\epsilon}E^{-1/4}$, including $-1/120$ at $E=N^{21/20}, b=N^{3/40}$ and $-1/48$ at fixed $b,E=N^{3/4}$.
3. **Re-optimized joint prefix.** [Proof](r21-joint-mixed-prefix.md), SHA-256 `2a39017805c98caa1a22d6298b1b66578820bbc8398a58ea820e4b0ff6db72d3`. For $b\le N^{1/40}$, $D^2<Q$ still holds and $e>Q/D$ gives the needed lower $V$ exponent $8/15$. Substituting $P_0=N^{3/50}, H=N^{11/50}, E=N^{5/6}$ into the uniform raw low-end bound gives six exponents $193/200,191/200,1151/1200,1157/1200,5/6,91/120$. No fixed-cutoff substitution is made: all original memberships use the actual $D(b)$, and the low/high split includes integer equality on the high side. The maximum raw exponent becomes $-1/100$ for signed rectangles after normalizing and summing the aliases, and $-1/200$ in HS; the high-end error is $-1/80$. At fixed $b$, the new raw maximum $24/25$ gives $-1/50$ in HS, and the same proofs use $Z\asymp_b N$ if one chooses the local cutoff for $0<b<1$. Earlier R17–R20 errors are smaller. This combines the exact mixed regions only at the smaller prefixes and does not estimate the remaining rough–rough term.

The true zero edge is included, and all off-diagonal alias signs and unit restrictions remain in force. There is no uniform sparsity assertion for the remaining terms, no result on the exterior crossings, and no proof or counterexample for S/R.
