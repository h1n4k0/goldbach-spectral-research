# Independent checks of Round 18

Baseline: [pinned Round 17](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/2f7c1e00c099b5f2332ca3bd8fa5856a58af862e/ROUND17.md). These are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review.

1. **True double-long product sector.** [Arithmetic proof](r18-arithmetic.md), SHA-256 `972378dc8a6e8820891236a46ece66666f9f82e0bf8b0c785d41cbcd674eede9`. Unit CRT imposes $(d,e)\mid h$ and gives density $(\varphi(N)/N)/[d,e]$; bounded-variation logarithmic weights on $n,m\ge1$ and the $O(Q\log(2Q))$ divisor pairs give the lattice error. For the density term, with $g=(d,e)$, squarefree terms have $d=ga$, $e=gc$, coefficient $\mu(a)\mu(c)/(gac)$ and $(c,agN)=1$. The allowed $c$ range is the exact half-open integer interval $(D/g,\min(Z/g,Q/(g^2a))]$. Convolution with the smooth-prime sequence supplies the restricted RH Mertens estimate with exponent loss $(agN)^\eta$; here $agN\le Z^2$. Partial summation handles separately the $1/c$ and $\log(c)/c$ tails. Both endpoint limits cancel in the difference, the short-end case $g>D$ uses a trivial bound, and $\sum_{g\mid h}g^{-1}\min(1,(D/g)^{-\sigma})\le D^{-\sigma}d(h)$ for $0<\sigma<1$. The true cumulative has no model at zero and vanishes if a coordinate is below $1/N$. The layer cake and divisor-count cap yield the Hilbert–Schmidt estimate with exponents $-1/40$ and $-7/480$ at the example parameters. This is a bound on a genuine portion of the double-long kernel under ζ RH, not the complementary region, not all of $T_N$.
2. **Actual mixed and doubly exterior tails.** [Operator proof](r18-operator.md), SHA-256 `eedc813920023bd766ab5246d08f1f96a07f35df0ab0e4874ccc9ff657a5ecb4`. Direct integration of the Volterra derivative gives the prime sample factor $-N^{-1/2}\Lambda(p)\chi(p)f(p/N)$, using a real test that isolates one unit prime and the actual supported unitary vector. Primitive inflation is unique, and cancellation of every pole in a finite completed combination forces the combination of logarithmic derivatives to be identically zero, by Hadamard, the common odd gamma factor, and the limit on the positive real line. Primes in the unit classes and orthogonality then force all coefficients to vanish. Compact smoothing on $(0,1)$ makes the frequency series absolutely summable, allowing sliding averaging uniform in its translate (at fixed $N$). Its test lies wholly on the high side of $H$. The inverse-height residue and Hardy weight are correctly normalized; the deterministic crossing is smaller. In the double tail a genuine sine subspace with dimension one greater than the rank of $P_N$ contains a test in the covariance nullspace after weight removal. The R17 symbol lower bound and derivative bound give the $L^3/\varphi(N)^2$ prefactor, and the benchmark remainder is smaller uniformly at $H\ge9L$. Both claims require local GRH(N); the first has no uniform residue/window bound as $N$ grows.
3. **Finite central-block moments.** [Spectral proof](r18-spectral.md), SHA-256 `50a667d4bfa220d0fcec7d16b8981a581fa2816f920864422ca20bfe89e39a90`. The unit condition removes the central atom, and reflection exactly rewrites the cumulative line as $F(x)+F(y)$. The resulting rank-two form (already implicit in Round 2) has norm comparable to $\|F\|_2$. Local upper sieve plus spacing of proper powers near $N/2$ bounds the variation of the actual signed measure on intervals at scale $N^{-\rho}$; this is not a global proper-power estimate misapplied to small intervals. Equal cells preserving signed mass yield cumulative error $O_\rho(SN^{-\rho})$ and a bounded derivative for the approximant. Sine Parseval on its derivative gives the claimed cosine tail, with the sine moments of the measure and the zeroth coefficient obtained by signed Fubini. The finite moments are not estimated. Local GRH(N) is used only to identify the arithmetic kernel with the actual block.

No result combines these three paths into a full spectral estimate, and Hypotheses S and R remain open.
