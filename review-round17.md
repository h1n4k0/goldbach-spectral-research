# Independent check of the two Round 17 reductions

Checks below concern distinct operators and assumptions. They are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review. Baseline: [pinned Round 16](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/5a453dbc6e4d2c8e14aa6893ceab28e979b9c176/ROUND16.md).

1. **Finite-prefix arithmetic.** For [the two-short proof](arithmetic-post-r16-mobius-longlong.md), SHA-256 `057765f7db0610fff07eabb908d15215d180e7bfeb9291c561be309fae2f7ee3`, CRT gives a class modulo $[d,e]$ precisely when $(d,e)\mid h$. All terms with $(de,N)>1$ drop out and unit inclusion–exclusion on the other terms supplies $\varphi(N)/N$ in the density and an error $O(2^{\omega(N)})$ per $(d,e)$. The log products have bounded size and variation on $n,m\ge1$, so the raw CRT error is $O_\epsilon(D^2N^\epsilon\log^2 Z)$. At primes dividing $h$ but not $N$, the common-divisor term is $pAB$, not $AB$; at $p\mid N$ both short divisor variables avoid $p$ and the numerator is 1. Multiplying the resulting quotient factors and the unit density gives $H_{h,N}(0,0)=\mathfrak S(h)$, including $p=2$. At the two stated negative real arguments the absolute Euler coefficient mass is $O_{\eta,\xi}(h^\xi)$, including primes dividing the shift. RH Mertens applies to each cutoff after convolution; the two linear errors and their product are controlled by that same absolute mass. This yields $O_\epsilon(Z^{5/6+\epsilon}/N)$ per normalized hard rectangle. The log marker is applied only on $n,m\ge1$; the completed constant-density model costs bounded raw length. Layer cake, the divisor-bound cap, and the true integrable zero edge give the full claimed Hilbert–Schmidt estimate. Expanding $\Lambda=\Lambda_D+R_D$ gives exactly $T_{\mathrm{prefix}}-L_{\mathrm{long,long}}=2C_{\mathrm{mixed}}-C_{\mathrm{short,short}}$ after deleting the diagonal. The model is deducted once, not twice. Global Dirichlet GRH is used only when taking the R16 mixed estimate and identifying the original operator; the two-short arithmetic step uses RH for $\zeta$. No bound on $L_{\mathrm{long,long}}$ follows.
2. **Doubly exterior deterministic model.** For [the stationary benchmark proof](operator-post-r15-stationary-tail.md), SHA-256 `2d635df95863399b9df7613c6c316e5d96c8d96bc2e93d05d288c5846a591bc4`, the exact scalar model is the [pinned Beta factorization](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/5a453dbc6e4d2c8e14aa6893ceab28e979b9c176/source-round8.md) with one $1/(1+s)$ per kernel variable. In the unitary physical log coordinates the two height inversions give $1/(2\pi)$; translation of the raw log by $\log N$ cancels $N^{-w}$ exactly. With $w=s+z$, the real change of heights has absolute Jacobian 1 and the contour residue contributes $2\pi$. The double-pole sign is negative. The terms proportional to $\min(x,y)-(x+y)/2$ and the constant $-1/2$ in the diagonal cancel with the inverse $(1+t^2)^{-2}$ term. This leaves precisely the stated stationary symbol including the bad-prime logarithms and the real digamma. On the shifted line, the uniform bad-prime bound is $\exp(C_\kappa L^\kappa)$; the squared integrand has separated exponents $-2+3\kappa<-1$. Plancherel and restored Hardy factors control the entire unbounded quadrant. Under local GRH(N), the diagonal remainder follows from RH for $\zeta$. The digamma lower bound and the fixed translated bump give the matching logarithmic-order norm at $h=\gamma L$. This is an approximation to $D_N+K_N$, not to $T_N$. Neither the corresponding cancellation by $P_N$ nor either crossing is estimated.

In particular these proofs do not combine into a full bound for the genuine Hardy residual and do not establish S/R.
