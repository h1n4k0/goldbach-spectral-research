# Independent checks for Round 23

These checks are internal mathematical review, not external peer review.

1. [Giant-prime identity and large proper cofactors](r23-source-giant-cofactor.md), SHA-256 `2c53750f688ad4c4d9979a20c7752327f776d46f424c42d15ee15ab8c098d548`. The giant is unique and every long divisor on that side is $pa$ with $a\mid r$. The other long divisor $e>D_*$ ensures $pae>Q$ even at $pD_*=Q$. The signed sum gives $-\Lambda(r)$ with no model term. The absolute per-line bound and exact zero at a raw coordinate below $1/N$ give the norm estimate; union masks are allowed by the absolute argument. The explicit four-prime witness uses global Dirichlet GRH only to obtain one prime in the required reduced class.
2. [Double-giant with a proper cofactor](r23-source-double-giant-proper.md), SHA-256 `21f68a9dedb3043138c50bd9563204a7c0431d91da83f4b3d4a32691a1aafc60`. For fixed cofactors the compatible primes lie along two affine forms on a one-dimensional lattice; away from the exceptional primes of $2rs|h|$, exactly two classes are excluded at each sieve prime. The interval scale and sieve level are uniform in the small-cofactor regime. The common-prime contribution to the weighted l.c.m. sum is $O(\log N)$; the separate-prime sum is no larger. Actual cumulative bounds and the $dx\,dy/(xy)$ measure produce the stated $c$, not a log-area loss in the main term. No assertion is made for a single giant with a small proper cofactor in this note.
3. [Prime-cofactor floor](r23-arithmetic-prime-cofactors.md), SHA-256 `459b3c99cad46ff6548b2cf68d4f3b6f4d916aded8d1d0f3b40888a3cc3591db`. The boxes are wholly within the original rough–rough high-product sector, and on the test support only the negative $n+m=N$ line survives. Common-prime pairs are negligible in the shell first moment. The uniform two-form sieve upper bound and bounded shell second moment of its elementary multiplicative factor imply the positive-proportion lower bound. The inverse cumulative test reproduces the exact atomic weight and the $1/N$ normalization. This is a lower bound on the selected summand only.
4. [Opposite signed forms on the same moduli](r23-arithmetic-compensation.md), SHA-256 `a4c65f5fffa7502a7ad3b1e4549ae539a77dc0bd578dd5d3f75be45ec9df4fa6`. With $P=Y^{1/4}$ the individual major error is $O_\epsilon(Y^{7/8+\epsilon})$ before normalization; the ordinary-modulus minor Fourier coefficient square sum is $O_\epsilon(Y^{11/4+\epsilon})$. Two exact prefix integrals and bounded diagonal multipliers on the shell $\ell^2$ space handle the moving smooth weights without a uniform exceptional-set claim. The unit correction is pointwise $O_f(X^{-1}\log^3 X)$, the actual negative alias gives $-2\pi$ times the normalized discrepancy, and Markov yields $O_{f,\epsilon}(X^{4/5+\epsilon})$ exceptions. The original wide-cutoff R22 comparison identifies the compensating rough–rough complement at fixed prefix.

The last step is global Dirichlet GRH conditional. It gives neither a norm bound nor compensation uniform over even $N$, and it is not evidence for a proof of S/R.
