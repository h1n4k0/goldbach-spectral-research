# Independent checks of the square-root-transition notes

These checks are internal mathematical review, not external peer review.

- [Critical-level reduction](spectral-critical-level-diagonal.md), SHA-256 `7a9b5aca302c2f796713acb335940a16430d99121c5ca0752d9893f4ced698a1`. The polylogarithmic shrinkage of $D$ absorbs the squarefree-completion GRH progression errors. The signed-rectangle reduction keeps the true moving edge and bounds the loss by counting the thin staircase bin, not by treating each possible residue as unrestricted. Above the transition, complementary divisors retain the strict moving endpoint, and the zeroth Euler factor vanishes. The proofs leave the lower ranges and the actual parity primitive open. The [supporting weighted reduction](spectral-post-r19-weighted-parity.md), SHA-256 `38c48b291455ceec6f6455385856443368a100173c2d72ee297a6f9750995f2d`, uses squarefree moduli in the needed error parameter; the auxiliary all-moduli shortcut is not substituted for it.
- [Maynard structured alternative](spectral-maynard-structured-critical.md), SHA-256 `a6da56445102c04a8bbb33b04660b094541963354beb06fa0fc1ee703312f007`. The factorization of actual squarefree-completion moduli fits Maynard III, Theorem 1.2, including the coherent residue $N$ across the third factor. Maximal prefixes follow by a polylogarithmic grid and monotonicity; proper powers are counted with divisor multiplicity, not once at full square-root cost for every modulus. The structured Euler inclusion–exclusion is legitimate and its small exponent separation follows from the stated modular nonresonance. This arithmetic result is unconditional; it removes an index class rather than an estimated share of the full mass.
- [Prime-sector floor](spectral-critical-prime-sector-floor.md), SHA-256 `bca8802f8dd106c851936ff2c944652eeb635d6f91ab63d1942094132f1547ec`. The literal squarefree-triple construction respects the strict cutoff, parity, central endpoints and exact unit condition. Brun–Titchmarsh and at most two eligible prime divisors per proper power give the uniform upper bound used with the first moment. Bounded mean singular series allows a fixed upper threshold on the retained positive-proportion family. The lower bound is for one sign only.
- [Non-nested exact-cutoff pairing](spectral-critical-nonnested-pairing.md), SHA-256 `7837337e434a1f284bc28d57738b52e41368aa92ab630151903b8bc08058375c`. The parity-selected $r(m)$ is independent of $d$, so $e=m/(rd)$ is injective, has positive Möbius sign, is not prime, and satisfies the strict $e<m/\sqrt N$ inequality. The weighted residual is a bounded logarithm minus $\log r$. For the original long piece with $\sqrt N<q<m/D$, complementing $q=m/e$ gives exactly $D<e<m/\sqrt N$ and logarithmic weight $\log e$, so the polylogarithmic choice of $Z$ genuinely retains both members. The at-most-one-factor upper sieve has dimension at most two and changes only the $p$-factor by at most $6/5$ after extracting the unique small prime. Prime powers, endpoint handling, and the log layer cake are all absorbed. The [nested predecessor](spectral-critical-opposite-parity-pairing.md), SHA-256 `79adbcae7d35f9dccda54ca3ff163c1c30755a04c7fda12a8a52874ea4585029`, supplies the simpler sieve check, not an estimate canceling the original dyad at exact cutoff.

The two removal strategies are alternative partitions. Their index sets can overlap; no sum of the claimed savings is implied. Neither a bound for the entire signed discrepancy nor S or R follows.
