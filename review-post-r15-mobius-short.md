# Independent check: pointwise centered short-divisor component

Reviewed [the release proof](arithmetic-post-r15-mobius-short.md), SHA-256 `7e0b6e9f32c6e6de497a67099be8d6136a61bbf2416d648baec3beaf0ea391ef`, using the [pinned physical kernel](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/79f04d767cd133273b5a516a1da1f086f7c32c5d/source-round2.md) and the [preceding prefix theorem](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/79f04d767cd133273b5a516a1da1f086f7c32c5d/ROUND15.md). This is an internal mathematical check, not outside peer review.

1. **The exact local main term.** Multiplying by $\zeta(1+s)$ separates a convergent correction. At zero, the factor at $2$ is $2$ because every shift $h$ is even; for $p>2$ the correction when $p\mid h$ has ratio $(p-1)/(p-2)$. Hence $F_h(0)=0$ and $F_h'(0)=+\mathfrak S(h)$. The convolution coefficients of $H_h(w-1)$ have absolutely summable $v^{-\sigma}$ weights for every fixed $\sigma>0$, uniformly with loss $h^\eta$. The [RH Mertens estimate](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/EM18/TitchmarshZeta.pdf) and partial summation give the stated two tails and identify both limits locally around zero. The logarithmic marker is $\log m-\log d$, so the sign of the surviving density is positive; the outer sum/difference signs remain unchanged.
2. **Progressions, unit restriction and ends.** For $(d,N)>1$ the unit term vanishes. If $(d,N)=1$ but $(d,h)>1$, a contributing Mangoldt factor must be a power of a single fixed bad prime; its weighted total is at most $O(\log^2 Z)$, and the cases with more than one such prime contribute zero. On reduced classes, deleting the powers of primes dividing $N$ costs only logarithms per divisor. [Lee's Corollary 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.13457#S1), the ordinary RH prime number theorem for $d=1$, and Chebyshev for $t<d\le D\ll\sqrt Z$ or bounded $t$ suffice for the auxiliary prefix bound. The Abel weight is first applied only where $m\ge1$; completing the continuum overlap to $m>0$ adds an integrable logarithmic endpoint error over a segment of bounded length. The weighted progression and Euler-truncation error scales are respectively $D\sqrt Z$ and $ZD^{-1/2}$ up to logarithms and subpowers; both are $Z^{5/6}$ at this $D$. The line measure is in the first normalized coordinate, so the division is by $N$.
3. **Genuine prefix norm and exact remainder.** The layer cake contributes $\sqrt{xy}$ to the rectangle bound. A separate absolute pointwise cap follows from $|\Lambda_D(m)|\le d(m)\log Z$ and the number of shifts per small-axis integer; no positive upper-sieve comparison for $\Lambda_D$ is needed. On $x<1/N$ the atomic term vanishes and the explicitly weighted zero edge is integrable. Summing $O(b)$ aliases, combining the two bounds, and taking the Hilbert–Schmidt square root gives $b^{11/12}N^{-1/12+\epsilon}$, or $N^{-7/480+\epsilon}$ at $b=N^{3/40}$. Symmetrization occurs only after exact diagonal subtraction. When this estimated centered piece is removed, the singular-series model has canceled exactly; the remaining signed long-divisor Möbius–prime sums are not estimated. The calculation is pointwise for the stated component under global Dirichlet GRH, and is neither a pointwise bound for the full prefix nor a claim on S/R.
