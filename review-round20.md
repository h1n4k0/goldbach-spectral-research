# Independent checks of the Round 20 arithmetic regions

Baseline: [pinned Round 19](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/8d23fc556b588675b44e718f26410d63a26e55ab/ROUND19.md). These are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review.

1. **Mixed good/rough sector at the larger prefix.** [Exact one-sided proof](r20-arithmetic.md), SHA-256 `597f7572654414e3ff28805758d3ccf0b25f5effb8b6475ec8337dc0c125689c`. The least-two-prime masks are disjoint and exhaustive for the good leg. Inclusion–exclusion keeps the original selected pair $L$ as a parameter, rather than re-selecting it from the expanded divisibility set. For each fixed base $a$ and other divisor $e$, all four orbit terms exist. When $a>D$ and $ae>Q$, both tests stay true and the two-prime difference of the logarithm is zero. When $ae\le Q$, the remaining high terms are exactly $Q<de\le Q(d,L)$; the raw larger band is used only to count the lattice errors. The unit CRT local split has independent compatibility conditions, and at fixed outer parameters the remaining sieve-free coordinate runs through a single interval with both exact endpoints. The reduced RH Mertens log tail applies with a polynomial-sized omitted-prime modulus; the local lcm absorbs the lower-cutoff factor. The true cumulative has no model or atoms at zero. The error bound is for this boundary portion, not its exact survivor $a\le D, ae>Q$. The latter forces $e>Q/D$; at the example parameters the exponent is $31/60$. The mixed sectors are separate from R19's both-good sector.
2. **Genuine balanced divisor box at the smaller prefix.** [Exact balanced-box proof](r19-source.md), SHA-256 `435790534ae5b28268b2d1973961a41a2c4b2da4619410bd2911f3d063cc78d2`. With $d=ga,e=gc$, the condition $g\mid j$ follows from $g\mid jN$ and $(g,N)=1$. Unit inclusion–exclusion is over all $r\mid N$, with $rg\mid h$ and no truncation. The progression in the normalized integer variable has residue $(\pm h/(rg))\bar a\bmod c$. Half-integer raw endpoints preserve the integer count, avoid the sawtooth jumps, and have only bounded-length density cost. For the [Vaaler approximation](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-1985-15349-2), the positive Fejér error is estimated using the absolute separated weights and its $O(H^{-1})$ coefficients. The nonzero dyads have frequency-vector norm $O(K^{-1/2})$ for both approximants. The [Bettin–Chandee primary theorem and Remark 1](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.00769#S1) allow the endpoint perturbation with derivative scale $X=O(1+KbN/(rg))$. The factor $g^{1/2}b^{1/2}K^{1/2}$ combines with the frequency norm; the remaining powers of $g$ are favorable. At $b=N^{1/100}$, $H=N^{3/200}$, $U,V\ll N^{501/1000}$, the raw error exponents are $0.987$, $0.9872$, and $0.951875$. Expanding the logarithmic cofactors leaves separated weights and bounded-variation raw functions. The signed density is smaller by the restricted RH Mertens tails on the actual divisor interval. Summing the $O(b)$ aliases after $1/N$ normalization gives exponent $-7/2500$ for the rectangle discrepancy and $-7/5000$ for the Hilbert–Schmidt bound after the true cumulative/Hardy transfer. Neither signs nor (possibly moving) raw endpoints were frozen across moduli.

The arithmetic assumptions are RH for $\zeta$ in both proofs; the Kloosterman estimate is an established unconditional input. Local GRH(N) supplies the original Hardy identification. No claim combines the smaller balanced-box result with the larger prefix to estimate the entire latter prefix, the exterior covariance, or S/R.
