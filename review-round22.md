# Independent checks of the Round 22 arithmetic results

These are internal mathematical checks, not outside peer review.

1. **Preserving the original wide cutoff.** [Proof](r22-compressed-wide-d.md), SHA-256 `5e07b9615e76cf2fc3fa3516587b6c3ad68c61ba513d6487734a4066ef76b50b`. Compression of the actual cumulative kernel to $x,y\le c$ only restricts the raw pair support; the earlier operator errors contract in Hilbert–Schmidt norm, and the $D_*,W,L,d/(d,L)$, unit and high-product labels are unchanged. The small-e proof uses $X\asymp cN$ for phases, endpoints and raw support, but uses the actual dyadic divisors $d\ll N^{43/120}\log^2N$ and $e\gg N^{31/60}$. The high-e proof fixes $d$ and $\ell$ in the original selected arithmetic and sums to $D_*XE^{-1/2}$ per raw line. With $E=N^{41/50}, P_0=N^{31/600},H=N^{41/200}$, the four leading small-e exponents and high-e exponent are all $73/75$; normalizing by $N$ and summing $O(c)$ aliases gives $-1/600$ and then the Hilbert–Schmidt exponent $-1/1200$. At fixed $c$, the largest raw exponent is $59/60$, giving $-1/120$. The endpoints partition the original integer divisors; compression with $c<1$ keeps the same wide labels. This is not an estimate of the rough–rough term and is not the Round 21 recut result.
2. **Exact adaptive cutoff.** [Proof](r22-arithmetic-adaptive.md), SHA-256 `d1594aa9bdfec6bf2738f1bebf98c0f52a8494584cf34d3bc5e01f54e56e7fa9`. With $d,\ell$ fixed, the real hard-coordinate tests and the original factor conditions cut a single $e$-interval. Of the three added sublevel tests in the adaptive cut, only $\sqrt e(h-\ell e)$ is nonmonotone on the positive range; its derivative $(h-3\ell e)/(2\sqrt e)$ makes the resulting subset at most two intervals. The signed masked progression bound applies on both. On an actual-position dyad $M\ge M_0$, the bound $\ell\le H/(M_0\sqrt E)$ cancels both the progression $\sqrt E$ loss and the $O(M_0)$ alias count. Summing dyads and exact local Boolean coefficients leaves raw rectangle mass $O_\epsilon(DHN^\epsilon)$. The unitary Hardy log kernel, true zero edge and square-root transfer give the announced norm bound. For the witness, the two integers are odd with disjoint prime factors; $3,5$ are exactly the selected small factors on the good leg, and the other leg is rough. The chosen prime windows give $3<D/a<5$ and $e>N^{3/4}$ by a fixed factor; exactly the $5a,15a$ terms remain in the specified orbit. Their signed weight is $-\log(3)\log(\ell)$ before the outer alias sign. The orbit belongs to the new cutoff and lies between the old wide-prefix ends. This is not an assertion about noncancellation across other orbits.

Both proofs use global Dirichlet GRH. They do not estimate the remaining signed long–long covariance, exterior crosses, or S/R.
