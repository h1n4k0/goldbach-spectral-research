# The canonical largest-prime carrier with complete prime alphabets and only the lower size gate

Use the genuine canonical largest-prime rows of the [count-unrestricted theorem](r101-source-canonical-largest-without-typical-tag.md). Keep its old prime $s$, actual hard physical windows, squarefree $N$-units, exact prime floors, seven signed long/product rows and Walsh convention. Write $L=\log X$, $\ell=\log L$, $T=L/\ell$, $0\le a,b\le A_0$ in a fixed finite floor box, and keep the true split $Z_0\le Z\le X^{\delta_0}$, $Z_0=\exp(2T\ell^{1/20})$, with the same fixed constants.

In each cofactor $h=sWU$, allow **every** prime in the lower interval $[e^{4T},Z]$ and the upper interval $(Z,\infty)$; both previously excluded neighborhoods are restored. There is no lower arity cap and no tagged count condition. Keep $W\le X^{\epsilon/2}$ and $U>1$, with $s,W,U$ otherwise as in the old factorization and the same conditions on the other arm. The actual distinguished prime is always $F=P^+(n)$ on $n=hF$ and similarly on the right. Denote the resulting genuine Walsh coefficients by $\widehat W_j^{\mathbf u}$.

Set $\tau=\log Z/L$, and let $\varrho_{+,\tau}$ be the positive real density from the convolution exponential of $\mathbf1_{\tau<v\le .7}\,dv/v$. This is used on the same compact upper-cofactor range as before; the cap $.7$ discards no relevant prime. Define the exact **new rooted normalizer**

$$
 \widehat{\mathcal C}_{X,Z}
    =\frac{|\mathcal J|}{\ell}
       \sum_{\substack{s,W\\\text{new lower alphabet, true size gate}}}
       \frac{\varrho_{+,\tau}(1/2-\log(sW)/L)}{sW},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal N}_{N,Z}
    =\Sigma(N)B_{\mathcal J}(N)
                    (\widehat{\mathcal C}_{X,Z}/|\mathcal J|)^2.     \tag{1}
$$

The seven phases $A_j(a,b)$ are exactly those of the [Walsh theorem](r96-source-walsh-two-full-and-dominance-switch.md), without any change in orientation or sign. Let $m=m_j(\mathbf u)$ be the number of actual surviving full signs, and put $v_0=1,\ v_1=2/3,\ v_2=4/3$.

**Theorem.** Uniformly $\widehat{\mathcal C}_{X,Z}\asymp X$. For

$$
 E_j^{\mathbf u}
   =\widehat W_j^{\mathbf u}
      -\mathbf1_{\mathbf u=\mathbf b_j}\widehat{\mathcal N}_{N,Z}A_j(a,b)
$$

at any fixed common $(a,b,Z)$, the squared target norm of the maximum over $j,\mathbf u$ with the indicated $m$ is $O(X^3\ell^{-2v_m})$. With any $r\in\{0,1,2\}$ free floor axes and a genuinely target-adaptive true split, the common maximal squared norm is $O(X^3\ell^{-2v_m/(r+2)})$. The other floors, if any, are common fixed values. One may take a finite union over the row/bit choices. This is a law for the specified genuine largest-prime support, not for a prescribed exceptional target, an unfiltered divisor sum, or Hypothesis S.

## 1. An actual collision forces precisely one upper prime

On the true squarefree canonical support the complete containing orbits vanish exactly as in the count-unrestricted theorem: each cofactor is composite since it contains $s$ and an upper factor, and $\Lambda(h)=0$. The true short labels and ordinary/containing signs are unchanged by the new alphabets. In all short rows the geometric bounds still give $\log h=L/2+O_{A_0}(T)$, and every short label lies below $M=\exp((24+2A_0)T)<Z_0$. The same statements hold on the right. Label primes thus remain below the true split. In particular the actual seven-row decomposition and its fixed positive all-label windows did not require an arity cap.

Use temporarily the rooted Fourier arm polynomials with every candidate near-square-root prime $F$ in the true hard windows and with genuine squarefree $h=sWU$. At this step allow nonlargest $F$ and allow $F\mid h$; the rooted coefficients are defined by their already-expanded signs and marks, not by an identity for nonsquarefree $hF$. On returning to the true row remove both bad types, including their possible overlap without multiplicity. A removed pair of factors $F,P$, where $P$ is a prime in $h$ with $P\ge F$, can include $P=F$ in the repeat case. Both factors are at least $\sqrt X e^{-A_0T-O(1)}$. The residual product in the hard physical window obeys

$$
             \frac{hF}{FP}\le e^{C T}<Z_0                       \tag{2}
$$

for a fixed $C$ depending only on the fixed floor box. Moreover $P>Z$ for all allowed splits. Hence $U=P$ is the sole upper factor, and all primes of $W$ lie in $[e^{4T},e^{CT}]$. This exact implication replaces the earlier zero-tag bound; no tag remains. The same argument bounds the candidate root multiplicity by two per physical integer, since three near-square-root factors exceed the window.

Dominate a removed arm positively by allowing all lower factors in this fixed log band and exactly one upper prime, with the same original physical window and hard bin if present, and the positive short-label mark. The lower labeled reciprocal mass of this polynomial is $O(1)$: its log-ratio band is fixed, and allowing all possible eligible label choices costs only a second occupation weight for each eligible prime and at most two choices for the old prime. The positive one-prime upper reciprocal density is $1/v=O(1)$ on the relevant compact range, with the same uniform hard-interval reciprocal PNT evaluation. Thus in a hard label bin of normalized width $\delta$ the integrated density is $O(\delta/\ell)$, since its cofactor log interval at fixed label and physical $x$ has normalized width at most $\delta/\ell$. The same bound controls its total variation in $x$ for the fixed bin width used below; moving clip endpoints costs $O(1/L)$. The old major and minor arguments for both genuine bin coordinates apply to this positive polynomial. Here the allowed upper prime is merely a restriction on the coefficient at fixed $h$. It has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4}\delta/\ell)$; the unbinned version has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4}/\ell)$. In particular the signed removed-arm polynomial in any bin satisfies the same norm bound, by coefficientwise absolute domination and the two-fold convolution identity for the fourth norm.

There is no arity loss obstructing these norms or the full rooted polynomials. In the active cofactor window $\omega(W)=O(\ell)$ from the least lower prime. The bounded log label cutoff permits only a fixed number of prime factors of any one label, giving $\ell^{O(1)}$ labels, not an exponential weight in $\ell$. Together with the two-root multiplicity this keeps the coefficient energy within $O(XL^{C'})$ for a fixed $C'$, as needed in the hard half-integer Perron and bilinear minor argument. The unrestricted weighted lower Euler estimates instead allow every eligible small-prime choice, costing only a bounded *relative* reciprocal product on the fixed log-ratio label band. These are different bounds for different steps.

## 2. Relative peeling by the actual mean logarithmic size

The signed/unsigned upper reciprocal comparisons of the [growing-residual theorem](r75-source-dynamic-giant-growing-rough-residual.md) now use the single interval $(\tau,.7]$ and no count selector. The same distinct-prime replacement gives arbitrary inverse-logarithmic accuracy on hard cofactor intervals and bounded-variation marks. Its density and Lipschitz norm on the compact range are $O(\tau^{-1})$ for either sign, by the single-coordinate marking identity. The positive lower labeled mass is $O(\ell\tau)$, also without any cap on count. The positive full arm therefore has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4})$; a bin of normalized width $\delta$ has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4}\delta)$ for the bin width used below.

The following positive comparison controls removing a chosen detachable lower band in the real density. Write $W=W_{\mathrm d}W_1$, with no arity gate. For its absolute envelope allow weights $1\le d_p\le2$ (label choices) and normalize the product $\prod_{p\ {\rm in\ band}}(1+d_p/p)$. The expected value of $\Delta=\log W_{\mathrm d}$ under that positive measure is

$$
     \sum_{p\ {\rm in\ band}}\frac{d_p\log p}{p+d_p}
                           \ll \log P_{\mathrm d},                \tag{3}
$$

where $P_{\mathrm d}$ is the top prime of the band. Thus one may discard $\Delta>\epsilon L/4$ in each comparison at relative cost $O(\log P_{\mathrm d}/L)$, by Markov. In the original term the true size gate ensures compact arguments even before this discard, and in the detached comparison the upper density is evaluated without $\Delta$, at the genuine retained size window. For the other values of $\Delta$, shifting the same real density argument by $\Delta/L$ costs a relative $O(\Delta/L)$ by the Lipschitz bound on the compact (or a fixed slight extension of it). Moving the true gate $\log(W_1W_{\mathrm d})\le\epsilon L/2$ to $\log W_1\le\epsilon L/2$ changes a strip where $\log W_1\ge\epsilon L/4$. To bound its relative positive reciprocal mass, expand $\log W_1$ as the sum of its prime logs and mark a prime. At fixed other primes, the marked reciprocal log sum over a log interval of length $\Delta$ is $O(1+\Delta)$ by Chebyshev and partial summation. Division by $\log W_1\gg L$ gives $O((1+\Delta)/L)$. This bound is valid including weak endpoints; it does not insert a prime below its allowed band. Averaging these costs with (3) gives relative error $O((1+\log P_{\mathrm d})/L)$. This proof replaces a worst-case arity truncation by the mean of the actual positive log envelope; the temporary exclusion of a large $\Delta$ is then released by the same Markov bound. The retained cutoff and overall real phase in the resulting detached expression do not refer to $W_{\mathrm d}$.

The argument applies to the two bin integrals for a full-sign arm, with the original real prime marks $\log(Fr)/\log F$ or $\log(h/z)/\log F$, and to their scaled $x$-derivative marks on a common clipped log interval. They are bounded, uniformly in the fixed label and true physical window. Thus apply the signed computation of the [unrestricted full-sign term](r98-source-tag-full-sign-stretched-cancellation.md), with its band now complete. If $Z\ge Z_*=\exp(T\ell^{1/3})$, detach all primes from $\exp((26+2A_0)T)$ through $Z_*$. They use no short label and the upper selector is unrestricted. Their signed-to-positive ratio is

$$
      \prod_{p\ {\rm in\ band}}\frac{1-1/p}{1+1/p}
                                  \ll \ell^{-2/3},
$$

by the reciprocal prime mass $\frac13\log\ell+O(1)$. The relative shift and size-strip cost just proved is $O(\log Z_*/L)=O(\ell^{-2/3})$. For $Z\le Z_*$, the signed upper measure is already the exact free convolution exponential, with density $-\rho(v/\tau-1)/v$ on the compact, by the same full-sign proof. Its Dickman factorial decay is smaller than any fixed negative power of $\ell$ since $\tau\le\ell^{-2/3}$. There is **no** excluded-band correction in this case. These estimates give $O(X^{3/4}\delta\ell^{-2/3})$ for the rooted full-sign fourth norm in either bin coordinate. The physical endpoint variation on the symmetric differences of two clipped intervals is bounded positively by $O(\ell/L)$, which is negligible at these scales. The old discrete arc and minor terms remain arbitrary inverse logarithmic for all allowed splits, with the coefficient bounds of Section 1.

## 3. Products and the exact matching phases

Use fine normalized bins of width $\delta=\ell^{-2}$ in the [two actual label coordinates](r89-source-four-actual-parity-phases.md). The hard Perron separation is interval-based; the narrower width introduces no inverse-width loss in the discrete estimates. For each individual signed row there are $O(\delta^{-2})$ accepted bin pairs and only $O(\delta^{-1})$ ambiguous ones, since the actual moving long/product thresholds shift by $O(1/T)=o(\delta)$ in the normalized coordinates. On ambiguous pairs keep all literal strict/weak tests and bound positively. They cost $O(X^{3/2}\delta)$ in target norm by the bin fourth norms. Restoring the temporary unit or fixed prime-floor comparisons costs only arbitrary inverse logarithmic error or $O(X^{3/2}\ell/L)$ as before, by the positive arm bounds and bounded root multiplicity.

On a fully accepted pair the signed arithmetic is a separated convolution. For each bin subtract its truly removed rooted terms *on that arm* to pass to the canonical, squarefree arm. Section 1 bounds the fourth norm of the subtraction by $O(X^{3/4}\delta/\ell)$. This is smaller than the signed full-arm bound of Section 2, so even after the subtraction an arm with one full sign has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4}\delta\ell^{-2/3})$. This avoids losing the two-full saving to an unbinned positive pair correction: after subtracting on each arm, use both resulting signed bounds in the pair product. An arm with no full sign has fourth norm $O(X^{3/4}\delta)$. Hölder and Parseval, then summing accepted and ambiguous bins, give $O(X^{3/2}\ell^{-2m/3})$ for the true one- and two-full channels.

In the matching case the actual projections remove both full signs, but retain both short signs. First evaluate the rooted matching sum. With the compact density $\varrho_{+,\tau}$, the accepted scalar comparison is exactly (6) of the two-coordinate proof. On a bin its principal density is $O(\delta)$. The real shift, mark and density variation errors in that proof are now $O(\delta/\ell+1/T)$: the first is the bin width times the uniform relative density and prime-mark perturbation, and the extra $1/T$ bounds the ordinary translated endpoint error. They have the same real transform bound with the factor $\min(X,|\beta|^{-1})$. Completing reduced numerators before absolute values, the same summable squarefree Ramanujan bound followed by summing bin products costs $O(X^{3/2}\ell^{-1})$; here $1/(T\delta)=o(\ell^{-1})$. Replacing accepted boxes by their limiting real areas has negligible $O(X^{3/2}\delta)$ error. This uses the same literal row geometry and seven separate phases, not another choice of divisor boundary.

Apply the [signed short Euler comparison](r97-source-matching-walsh-raw-euler-rate.md) to each of these bounded Lipschitz two-variable areas, with the new lower alphabets. Only primes below a fixed $e^{A T}$ (and the old prime) can enter the short labels, since each area vanishes outside the fixed short-coordinate box. Peel that fixed log band in the real expression. The comparison (3) now costs only $O(T/L)=O(\ell^{-1})$, with no count-gate loss. The original signed short atoms and their convolution exponential are unchanged: this band was already inside the old allowed alphabet; label eligibility, old-prime law and short signs are the same. The analogous unsigned comparison without labels gives the same detached factor $\widehat{\mathcal C}_{X,Z}/|\mathcal J|$ from (1), to error $O(\ell^{-1})$; neither one is replaced by a new limiting constant. The two-arm area scalar is thus $(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{X,Z}/|\mathcal J|)^2 A_j(a,b)+O(\ell^{-1})$, including each separate mixed and double row with its inherited sign. The real positive comparison gives the upper bound on (1), and inclusion of all earlier allowed lower and upper profiles gives its positive lower bound from the preceding largest-prime theorem. Multiplication by $\Sigma(N)B_{\mathcal J}(N)$ uses $\|\Sigma\|_2\ll\sqrt X$. Restoring canonical roots in this matching comparison costs $O(X^{3/2}/\ell)$ by the unbinned positive correction of Section 1. This proves all fixed-node claims.

For instance the new support genuinely includes true canonical operands with more than $2\log\ell$ lower primes and with a formerly excluded prime in $[V_X/2,2V_X]$. On one leg choose that prime together with $\lceil3\log\ell\rceil$ distinct primes near $e^{(9/2)T}$ for $W$; take a distinct old prime $s$. These are below $Z_0$ and $\log W=o(L)$. Choose an upper prime $U$ making $h=sWU$ of order $\sqrt X/4$ in a small proportional interval, and a genuine largest prime $F$ near $2\sqrt X$ with $hF$ interior to $\mathcal J$. The prime number theorem supplies these choices, with fixed proportional subintervals and all distinct factors. A right mate can be chosen in the same physical interval with disjoint primes and $W'=1$. Their actual even sum has both floors at zero, is a genuine squarefree unit pair, and the ordinary pair $d=h,e=k$ passes all three original cuts. This witness is not in the previously allowed alphabets or lower arity range.

## 4. Common maximum for the same true support

Use the positive gap proof of the [marked split theorem](r83-source-full-polynomial-adaptive-split.md), with the fixed rooted base $Z_0$ and the global prime and cofactor windows. The canonical root and the squarefree test are intrinsic on the actual support. A lost actual operand in a split gap needs both a genuine gap prime and failure of the remaining **size** gate at its upper endpoint. There is no arity-failure alternative. At the fixed base, all marked primes are in the original upper product; the upper selector is unrestricted and both missing-neighborhood masks have simply been removed. The positive tilted argument uses the same complete reciprocal upper bound without exclusions, and the polynomial bound on the actual label count from Section 1 suffices on the minor arcs. Thus for an upper split gap of exponent length $d$ with endpoint ratio at most two the old norm bound is $O_A(X^{3/2}(d+L^{-A}))$. The raw [unmarked subpower estimate](r82-source-target-adaptive-subpower-split.md) in the single initial gap below $\tau_*=(\epsilon/16)\log\log\ell/\log\ell$ uses just its size term, at most $\ell^{-1}$, giving norm $O(X^{3/2}/\ell)$. Genuine floor gaps have the same positive prime-slab norm $O(X^{3/2}(d+\ell/L))$. The bounds again use global positive envelopes, independent of the other chosen coordinates.

For (1), calibrate by the exact fixed positive reference prime interval with rooted weight $\log F$, as in the prior largest-prime theorem, without imposing the actual canonical veto for this calibration alone. The compact Lipschitz density gives the same zero-frequency $O(X/\ell)$ error uniformly. The true reference lost terms obey precisely the same size-only gap criteria, so the changes of the exact rooted normalizer are $O(X(d+\ell^{-1}))$ on upper gaps and $O(X/\ell)$ in the initial gap. No calibration is used for the zero-comparator channels.

For fixed $m$ mesh the free floors and the split exponent above $\tau_*$ with spacing $\eta=\ell^{-2v_m/(r+2)}$, treating the single initial interval separately. These widths are $o(\tau_*)$. Sum squared positive envelopes by axis alone, using their independence from the other coordinates. The squared gap cost is $O(X^3(\eta+\ell^{-2}))=O(X^3\eta)$. For $m=0$ the exact comparator changes pointwise by $O(X|\Sigma(N)|(\eta+\ell^{-1}))$ using the Lipschitz phases, which costs $O(X^3(\eta+\ell^{-1})^2)$ without a grid factor. There are $O(\eta^{-(r+1)})$ nodes, each with the fixed squared error already proved, so their total cost is $O(X^3\eta^{-(r+1)}\ell^{-2v_m})=O(X^3\eta)$. This gives the stated actual common maximum, without changing the hard moving tests.
