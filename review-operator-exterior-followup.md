# Independent checks of the exterior follow-up notes

These checks are internal mathematical review, not external peer review.

- [Uncentered direction](r22-operator-clean.md), SHA-256 `c03c02fb973433aef6251d338b983bb9b706dc9548673737210cf660051d244d`. The high profile integrates to $W(\max(Y,m/N))$, and raw low rescaling gives precisely $2\pi\sqrt{YW(Y)}/\sqrt N$. For a unit residue the ordinary prime and proper-power estimates hold uniformly over the full infinite exterior. Low and high dyadic cuts of the smoothed prime sum have errors $O_\gamma(L/\sqrt N)$ and $O_\gamma(S/\sqrt N)$, which are $o(\delta_N)$; the full direction is not estimated at that accuracy. The squared-denominator Mellin transform makes the actual residue sum absolutely convergent, with the positive residue sign for the negative Volterra atom. Odd orthogonality and Schur's test justify the low synthesis bound and subsequent ordinate cutoff. The broader proper-power deletion is limited to its explicitly stated smaller raw prefix or bounded-$\omega(N)$ regimes. The clean file restores TeX without modifying the checked formulas.
- [Separated and early covariance sectors](r24-operator.md), SHA-256 `391a824ccd0c77c8a684c6cdeb43c2f273ffffa7ec84043028188390d95f2c73`. For ordered indices $k<\ell$ the actual bridge is $(1-W_k/W_{-1})W_\ell$. Both monotone-weighted sums on an ordered dyadic rectangle retain the true residue increments and are controlled by vector Abel summation. These estimates apply also to partially truncated rectangles, so absolute trace summation over the selected rectangles agrees with simultaneous covariance cutoffs. Summing the second dyadic variable geometrically leaves the slow damping $\alpha=1/\log N$ in the first variable; its summation with $m$ logs gives $O(L^{m+1})$, including the two log factors in the GRH–GRH case. The resulting three errors are $o(\delta_N^2)$ even for unbounded heights in physical space. The early map is controlled in HS before taking its cross products with the full map in trace norm. This only gives its claimed fixed inverse-log deletion and makes no comparable-dyad cancellation claim.

These are controls on separate constituents of the microscopic-to-exterior crossing, not a full operator estimate or a result about either S or R.
