# Independent check: decaying actual kernel and larger full prefix

Reviewed [the exact proof](operator-post-round14-decaying-prefix.md), SHA-256 `2463c9877c66a018b7fcd8865f0ef8cc10405bee378a00c5a5d4bfe760366e88`, against [the actual physical kernel](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/c7e28563c459509a1a14676888bfc63cdf2bb1d8/source-round2.md) and the [pinned Round 14 unequal-scale estimate](https://github.com/h1n4k0/goldbach-spectral-research/blob/c7e28563c459509a1a14676888bfc63cdf2bb1d8/operator-post-round13-asymmetric-exterior.md). This is an internal proof check, not external peer review.

1. **Actual character and diagonal terms.** In Lebesgue physical coordinates, the character vector is $-\sqrt{2\pi}\,r_N(x)/N$ times $\sum_{n\le Nx}\Lambda(n)\chi(n)\sqrt n$; the normalized odd average gives the true covariance, by the difference of the two residue indicators. All characters here are nonprincipal. [Montgomery–Vaughan, Theorem 13.7](https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/MNTI/17.0_pp_419_451_Conditional_estimates.pdf), or its proof at height $t$ using the uniform primitive zero count and the finite Euler correction, gives a bound uniform also when $t<q$. That is important for both the physical character term and the auxiliary majors on small dyads: the older simplifying $q\le\sqrt W$ is not assumed. Abel summation gives the $L^2/\sqrt x$ character-vector cap. In the exact diagonal, Chebyshev gives a sum of $n\Lambda(n)^2$ of size $O(t^2\log(2+t))$, which yields $O(L/\sqrt{xy})$.
2. **Exact cancellation of the continuum model.** The squarefree divisor coefficients for $\mathfrak S(kN)/\mathfrak S(N)$ have partial sum $O(\log(2+t))$, by the Euler bound and Mertens, uniformly in $N$. Inserting floors and bounding the divisor tail gives the stated linear density with logarithmic remainder. For fixed $u$, the two positive charts $v=u+k$ and $v=u-k>0$ cover the parts of $0<v\le y$ above and below $u$. The negative chart $v=k-u>0$ covers the whole interval once. Their continuous integrals have the same $\sqrt v$ weight and unit Jacobian; thus they cancel exactly. Each discrete chart, viewed as a function of the line parameter, has variation $O(\sqrt y)$ after restricting to $0<v\le y$. Abel summation and then integration in $u$ with weight $\sqrt u$ give $O(\mathfrak S(N)x^{3/2}\sqrt y\log(2+x+y))$ for $x\le y$. After multiplying by the actual Hardy factors the model satisfies the same decaying kernel cap as required. No cancellation of unmodeled prime pairs is used in this pointwise step.
3. **Dyadic transfer and exponents.** A contributing dyad with $U\le V$ has $V\ge1/4$ by support $x+y\ge1$. The chosen raw cutoffs cover all $O(V)$ aliases and the short leg remains at least a constant times $X^{3/4}$. The weighted layer cake gives the selected absolute-kernel sum $O(C_E/(xy))$, measured in Lebesgue $dx\,dy$. Multiplying by $O(L^4/\sqrt{xy})$ gives an integral factor $O((UV)^{-1/2})$, not the older pair of logarithms at this step. The resulting minor term is $\sqrt M X^{3/20+\epsilon} V^{23/20}$ up to logs. At $Y=X^{3/40}$ the four linear exponents are $-13/400,-77/400,-3/10,-7/40$ for the mixed major term, series/completed integral, unit/restoration and actual zero edge, respectively, before arbitrarily small subpowers and logs. Each is strictly below $\tau^2=X^{-1/200}$. Summing dyads for the same selected set and squaring after dividing by $\sqrt M$ gives $193/400$; restricting the dyads to the old high–high block gives $17/40$.

The new theorem concerns the full finite prefix compression of the true operator for most members of the specified doubled-square dyad, under global Dirichlet GRH. It uses no additional fixed-weight fourth-moment theorem, does not provide exterior or pointwise full-operator control, and makes no claim on S/R.
